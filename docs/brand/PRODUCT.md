# Product Overview

## What is Dad Coach?

Dad Coach is an AI-powered coaching platform that helps fathers become more intentional, confident, and connected parents. Through personalized guidance delivered via conversational AI, Dad Coach meets fathers where they are and helps them grow — one small step at a time.

## Problem Statement

Fatherhood lacks structured support. While mothers benefit from extensive communities, apps, and professional networks, fathers often navigate parenting in isolation. Many want to be better dads but don't know where to start, feel overwhelmed by conflicting advice, or struggle to find resources that speak to their experience without judgment.

The result: disengagement, guilt, and missed opportunities to build strong relationships with their children during the years that matter most.

## Solution

Dad Coach provides personalized, AI-driven coaching that adapts to each father's unique family situation, goals, and schedule. The platform delivers:

- Bite-sized, actionable guidance that fits into a busy life
- Progress tracking through a belt-based achievement system
- Contextual coaching that remembers family details and evolving needs
- A judgment-free space to explore parenting challenges
- Evidence-based strategies drawn from child development research

See [AI_PHILOSOPHY.md](./AI_PHILOSOPHY.md) for how the AI coaching model works.

## Target Users

- **Primary:** Fathers aged 28–45 with children aged 0–12
- **Secondary:** Expectant fathers, co-parents, and father figures

See [TARGET_AUDIENCE.md](./TARGET_AUDIENCE.md) for detailed audience analysis and [PERSONAS.md](./PERSONAS.md) for representative user profiles.

## Core Pillars

1. **Personalization** — Every father's journey is unique. Coaching adapts to family context, child age, and personal goals.
2. **Progression** — Growth is visible and celebrated. The belt system provides structure without pressure.
3. **Accessibility** — Coaching fits into real life. Short interactions, flexible timing, multiple channels.
4. **Trust** — Evidence-based guidance delivered with warmth. Never manipulative, never judgmental.
5. **Connection** — The ultimate goal is stronger father-child relationships, not app engagement.

## Success Metrics

- Father engagement consistency (weekly active coaching interactions)
- Self-reported confidence in parenting decisions
- Progression through belt milestones
- Retention at 30, 60, and 90 days
- Quality of father-child interactions (self-assessed)

See [NORTH_STAR.md](./NORTH_STAR.md) for the single metric that guides all prioritization.
