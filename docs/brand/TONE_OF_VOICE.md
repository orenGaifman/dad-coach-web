# Tone of Voice

This document defines how Dad Coach communicates with fathers across all touchpoints — from AI coaching conversations to notifications, error messages, and marketing copy.

For the AI-specific communication approach, see [AI_PHILOSOPHY.md](./AI_PHILOSOPHY.md).

## Writing Style

### We Are

- **Warm** — like a knowledgeable friend, not a clinical expert
- **Direct** — clear and efficient, respecting the reader's time
- **Encouraging** — always forward-looking, always empowering
- **Grounded** — practical and specific, never vague or fluffy
- **Honest** — acknowledging difficulty without sugarcoating

### We Are Not

- Preachy or lecturing
- Overly casual or jokey
- Clinical or academic
- Patronizing or condescending
- Aggressive or pushy

### Voice Principles

- Use "you" and speak directly to the father
- Prefer short sentences and active voice
- Use everyday language — no jargon unless explaining a concept
- Be specific: "Try 5 minutes of floor play before dinner" beats "Spend quality time with your child"
- Acknowledge reality: fathers are tired, busy, and doing their best

## Examples

### Notifications

**Good:**
> "Bedtime tonight? Try asking your daughter about the best part of her day. One question, big connection."

**Bad:**
> "Don't forget to engage with your child today! Studies show that fathers who interact daily have better outcomes."

**Good:**
> "You've been consistent for two weeks. That's real momentum."

**Bad:**
> "You're on a 14-day streak! Don't lose it now! 🔥"

---

### Achievement Messages

**Good:**
> "Green belt earned. You've shown up for your family consistently — that's what growth looks like."

**Bad:**
> "CONGRATULATIONS! 🎉🎉🎉 You CRUSHED IT! You're now a Level 3 Super Dad!"

**Good:**
> "New milestone: You've completed 10 coaching conversations. Each one is an investment in your family."

**Bad:**
> "Achievement unlocked! Keep grinding to unlock more rewards!"

---

### Empty States

**Good:**
> "Nothing here yet. When you're ready, your first coaching session takes about 3 minutes."

**Bad:**
> "You haven't started yet. Get going — your kids are counting on you!"

**Good:**
> "Your progress will appear here as you engage with coaching. No rush."

**Bad:**
> "This page is empty because you haven't done anything yet."

---

### Error Messages

**Good:**
> "Something didn't work on our end. Your progress is safe — try again in a moment."

**Bad:**
> "Error 500: Internal Server Error. Please contact support."

**Good:**
> "We can't connect right now. We'll be back shortly."

**Bad:**
> "Network error! Check your connection and retry."

---

### AI Coaching Tone

**Good coaching response:**
> "Sounds like mornings are the hardest right now. That makes sense — everyone's rushed and tired. What if we focused on just one calm moment? Even 30 seconds of eye contact before you leave can change the tone of the whole day."

**Bad coaching response:**
> "Research indicates that morning routines are crucial for child development. You should establish a consistent 15-minute connection ritual each morning before leaving for work. Here are 7 steps..."

**Good follow-up:**
> "How did that feel? Even if it didn't go perfectly, you tried — and that matters."

**Bad follow-up:**
> "Did you complete the task? Remember, consistency is key to achieving your parenting goals."

## Channel-Specific Notes

### In-App

- Concise, scannable, action-oriented
- One idea per screen
- Progressive disclosure for detail

### Notifications

- Maximum 2 per day
- Always provide value or encouragement
- Never guilt-based
- Easy to act on or dismiss gracefully

### Email

- Warm, personal, brief
- One clear purpose per message
- Respect inbox — only send when genuinely useful
