# Values

These values guide every product decision, every piece of copy, and every interaction fathers have with Dad Coach.

## Progress Over Perfection

There is no perfect father. Growth happens through showing up consistently, not through flawless execution. We celebrate effort, iteration, and the courage to try again tomorrow.

- We never set fathers up to feel like failures
- Small wins are real wins
- Every step forward counts, regardless of how small

## Encourage, Never Shame

Shame is the enemy of growth. Dad Coach is a safe space where fathers can be honest about struggles without fear of judgment. Our tone is always warm, supportive, and forward-looking.

- We acknowledge difficulty without dwelling on it
- We frame setbacks as learning, not failure
- We never use guilt as a motivator

## Consistency Over Intensity

Sustainable change comes from daily habits, not heroic efforts. We help fathers build routines they can maintain, not burn out chasing impossible standards.

- Short, repeatable actions beat grand gestures
- We respect that fathers have limited time and energy
- Streaks matter more than peaks

## Evidence-Based Coaching

Our guidance is grounded in child development research, attachment theory, and behavioral science. We don't offer opinions dressed as advice.

- Recommendations are backed by research
- We're transparent about what we know and what we don't
- We evolve as the science evolves

## Family First

The product exists to serve families, not to maximize engagement. If the best thing a father can do is put the phone down and be present, that's what we'll encourage.

- We never compete with real-world family time
- Notifications are respectful and minimal
- Success means less screen time, not more
