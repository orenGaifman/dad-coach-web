# North Star

## The Single Metric That Matters

**More present moments between fathers and their children.**

Everything we build, every decision we make, every feature we prioritize should ultimately increase the quantity and quality of intentional moments fathers share with their kids.

## North Star Metric

**Weekly intentional interactions** — the number of times per week a father consciously engages with his child using guidance, habits, or awareness developed through Dad Coach.

This metric captures:

- Whether coaching translates into real-world behavior
- Whether the product drives action, not just consumption
- Whether fathers are building sustainable habits

## What This Means in Practice

### We succeed when:

- A father puts the phone down and gets on the floor with his kid
- A bedtime routine becomes a connection ritual instead of a battle
- A difficult conversation happens because a dad felt prepared
- A missed moment leads to self-compassion instead of guilt
- A father feels more capable today than he did last month

### We fail when:

- Fathers spend more time in the app than with their children
- Coaching creates anxiety instead of confidence
- Progress feels performative rather than genuine
- The product becomes another obligation in an already full day
