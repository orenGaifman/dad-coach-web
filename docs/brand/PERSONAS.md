# Personas

These personas represent the primary users of Dad Coach. They are fictional but grounded in real patterns observed in the target audience.

---

## Persona 1: Amit — The Overwhelmed New Dad

### Background

- Age: 32
- Occupation: Software engineer at a mid-size tech company
- Location: Central Israel
- Education: B.Sc. Computer Science

### Family Situation

- Married, first child (daughter, 8 months old)
- Wife returned to work; both share parenting responsibilities
- Extended family nearby but advice often feels outdated

### Daily Routine

- Wakes at 6:30, handles morning feed
- Commutes and works 8:30–17:30
- Arrives home for bath time and bedtime routine
- 1–2 hours of personal time after baby sleeps

### Biggest Parenting Challenges

- Doesn't know what's "normal" for his daughter's development
- Feels clumsy during play — unsure what activities are appropriate
- Wife seems to intuitively know what to do; he feels a step behind
- Sleep deprivation affecting patience and presence

### Technology Comfort

High. Uses apps for fitness, finance, and productivity. Expects clean, modern interfaces.

### Goals

- Feel confident handling his daughter alone
- Build rituals that strengthen their bond
- Understand developmental milestones without medical jargon

### Fears

- Missing critical bonding windows
- Being seen as the "helper" parent rather than an equal
- Repeating his own father's emotional distance

### Motivations

- Wants his daughter to feel safe and loved with him
- Driven by self-improvement mindset from his career
- Sees engaged fatherhood as a core part of his identity

## Persona 2: David — The Stretched-Thin Father of Three

### Background

- Age: 41
- Occupation: Regional sales manager
- Location: Suburbs, commutes 45 minutes each way
- Education: MBA

### Family Situation

- Married, three children (ages 3, 7, and 10)
- Wife works part-time; primary logistics coordinator for the family
- Eldest child showing behavioral challenges at school

### Daily Routine

- Up at 6:00, out the door by 6:45
- Long workdays with travel 2–3 days per month
- Home by 18:30, helps with homework and dinner
- Weekends packed with activities and errands

### Biggest Parenting Challenges

- Each child needs different things at different stages
- Eldest son is pulling away — doesn't know how to reconnect
- Feels guilty about work travel and missed moments
- Defaults to discipline over connection when stressed

### Technology Comfort

Moderate. Uses work tools and WhatsApp extensively. Doesn't explore new apps unless clearly valuable.

### Goals

- Reconnect with his eldest son before the teenage years
- Be more patient during high-stress family moments
- Create meaningful one-on-one time with each child

### Fears

- His children remembering him as absent or angry
- Damaging his relationship with his eldest permanently
- Burnout affecting both work and family

### Motivations

- Deep love for his family despite the chaos
- Remembers his own father being distant and wants to break the cycle
- Colleagues who seem to "have it together" inspire him to try harder

## Persona 3: Yoni — The Intentional First-Timer

### Background

- Age: 29
- Occupation: UX designer at a startup
- Location: Urban apartment
- Education: B.Des. Visual Communication

### Family Situation

- Partner is 7 months pregnant with their first child
- Unmarried but committed and cohabitating
- Both sets of parents live abroad

### Daily Routine

- Flexible work hours, mostly remote
- Spends evenings preparing the home for the baby
- Reads parenting content actively — sometimes too much
- Active in online communities for expecting parents

### Biggest Parenting Challenges

- Information overload — can't distinguish good advice from noise
- Anxiety about doing things "right" from day one
- No local family support network
- Partner's expectations feel high and undefined

### Technology Comfort

Very high. Early adopter. Appreciates thoughtful design and personalization.

### Goals

- Feel prepared before the baby arrives
- Build a parenting approach together with his partner
- Start with good habits rather than correcting bad ones later

### Fears

- Being unprepared for the reality of a newborn
- Post-birth relationship strain with his partner
- Not knowing when something is actually wrong vs. normal

### Motivations

- Sees fatherhood as a creative challenge — wants to design his approach
- Values intentionality in all areas of life
- Wants to be the kind of dad who is fully present from day one
