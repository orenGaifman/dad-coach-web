# Vision

## What Success Looks Like

### In 3 Years

Dad Coach is the go-to coaching companion for engaged fathers. Thousands of dads use it weekly to navigate parenting challenges, track their growth, and maintain consistency. The belt system is recognized as a meaningful marker of parenting investment.

### In 5 Years

Dad Coach is a household name among new and expecting fathers. It's recommended by pediatricians, parenting educators, and family therapists. The platform supports fathers across all stages — from pregnancy through adolescence — and has measurably improved father-child relationship quality at scale.

### In 10 Years

Dad Coach has helped redefine what fatherhood support looks like. The data and insights generated have contributed to research on paternal engagement. A generation of children has benefited from more present, confident fathers. The platform has expanded globally, adapted culturally, and become part of how society supports families.

## The World We're Building

A world where:

- Every father has a patient coach in his pocket
- Parenting growth is celebrated the same way fitness progress is
- Children grow up with fathers who are intentionally present
- The "absent father" narrative is replaced by stories of consistent, supported dads
- Asking for parenting help is normalized for men

## Guiding Principle

We measure our success not by app metrics, but by the quality of real-world moments between fathers and their children.
