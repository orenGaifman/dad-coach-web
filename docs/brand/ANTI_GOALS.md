# Anti-Goals

## What We Will Not Build

Anti-goals are as important as goals. They define the boundaries that protect the product's integrity and the trust fathers place in us. These are not "nice to haves" — they are hard lines.

## We Will Not Maximize Screen Time

Dad Coach succeeds when fathers spend *less* time on screens and more time with their children. We will never optimize for session length, daily opens, or time-in-app as success metrics.

## We Will Not Gamify Fatherhood

The belt system represents genuine growth, not points in a game. We will not add leaderboards, competitive elements, streaks that punish absence, or rewards that exist solely to drive engagement.

## We Will Not Create Dependency

The goal is a confident father, not a father who can't make a decision without consulting the app. Coaching should build autonomy over time. A father who stops using Dad Coach because he no longer needs it is our greatest success.

## We Will Not Replace Professional Help

Dad Coach is coaching, not therapy. We will never position the product as a substitute for mental health professionals, pediatricians, or family counselors. We recognize our boundaries and refer out clearly.

## We Will Not Shame or Guilt

No notification will ever imply a father is failing. No empty state will pressure action. No message will use a child's wellbeing as leverage to drive engagement.

## We Will Not Sell Personal Data

Fathers share vulnerable, personal information with Dad Coach. That data exists solely to improve their coaching experience. It will never be sold, shared for advertising, or used against their interests.

## We Will Not Prescribe One Way

There is no single correct way to be a good father. We will never impose a specific parenting philosophy, cultural norm, or family structure as the "right" approach.

## We Will Not Sacrifice Trust for Growth

Growth that comes at the cost of user trust is not growth. We will not implement dark patterns, misleading copy, hidden costs, or manipulative retention tactics — regardless of business pressure.

## We Will Not Over-Notify

Notifications are a privilege, not a right. We will not abuse push notifications to drive opens. Every notification must provide genuine value or encouragement that could not wait.

## We Will Not Build Features Without Purpose

Every feature must trace back to the [North Star](./NORTH_STAR.md): more present moments between fathers and their children. Features that serve product metrics but not fathers will not ship.

---

## How to Use This Document

When evaluating a feature, campaign, or product decision, ask:

1. Does this violate any anti-goal?
2. Does this serve the father or the business first?
3. Would we be comfortable if every user read the reasoning behind this decision?

If any answer raises doubt, the decision needs rethinking.
